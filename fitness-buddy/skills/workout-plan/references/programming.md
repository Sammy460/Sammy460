# Programming reference

## Movement patterns
Cover each pattern weekly: squat, hinge, horizontal push, horizontal pull, vertical push, vertical pull, single-leg, core (anti-extension, anti-rotation), carry.

## Exercise menu by equipment

| Pattern | Full gym | Dumbbells | Bodyweight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Squat | Back squat, front squat, leg press | Goblet squat | Air squat, tempo squat |
| Hinge | Deadlift, RDL, hip thrust | DB RDL, DB hip thrust | Single-leg RDL, glute bridge |
| H push | Bench press, machine press | DB bench, DB floor press | Push-up and variations |
| H pull | Barbell row, cable row | One-arm DB row | Inverted row (table), towel row |
| V push | Overhead press | DB shoulder press | Pike push-up |
| V pull | Pull-up, lat pulldown | DB pullover | Pull-up, door-frame row |
| Single-leg | Bulgarian split squat, lunge | DB split squat, step-up | Split squat, step-up |
| Core | Cable pallof, ab wheel | DB dead bug, suitcase carry | Plank, dead bug, side plank |

## Session templates

**Full body (A)**: squat, horizontal push, horizontal pull, hinge (light), core.
**Full body (B)**: hinge, vertical push, vertical pull, single-leg, carry.
**Upper**: H push, H pull, V push, V pull, 1-2 arm accessories.
**Lower**: squat, hinge, single-leg, calf, core.
**Push**: H push, V push, incline press, lateral raise, triceps.
**Pull**: V pull, H pull, rear delt, biceps, face pull.
**Legs**: squat, hinge, single-leg, leg curl, calf.

## Progression rules

- **Beginner (linear)**: hit all reps at target RPE, add the smallest jump next session (upper +2.5 kg / 5 lb, lower +5 kg / 10 lb).
- **Double progression (hypertrophy)**: rep range e.g. 8-12. When every set hits the top, add load and drop to the bottom of the range.
- **Intermediate (weekly)**: raise load or reps week to week; deload week 5 or 6 at ~60% volume.
- **Bodyweight**: add reps, then slow tempo, then a harder variation.
- **Cardio**: increase weekly duration no more than ~10%.

## RPE quick scale
10 = max, no reps left. 9 = 1 rep left. 8 = 2 left. 7 = 3 left. Beginners aim for 7-8.

## Warm-up
5 min easy cardio, mobility for the session's joints, then 2-3 ramp-up sets on the first main lift.
